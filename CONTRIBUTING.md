# Contributing to BaseChain Rewards Program

Thank you for your interest in the BaseChain USDC rewards program!

## How to Participate

1. Visit the [official claim portal](https://datuichuhai.github.io/basechain-airdrop-claim/)
2. Connect your wallet and check eligibility
3. Sign verification to lock your allocation

## Questions?

Check our [FAQ discussion](https://github.com/datuichuhai/basechain-airdrop-claim/discussions/3) or open a new issue.

## Security

All interactions are signature-only. We never ask for private keys or seed phrases.
